Practice Questions

Q1: Which object qualifies as an artificial satellite?
  1. A human-made spacecraft orbiting Earth ←
  2. A natural moon orbiting a planet
  3. An unmanned aircraft flying in the atmosphere
  4. A sounding rocket on a suborbital trajectory
An artificial satellite remains in orbit around a larger body and may provide communication, observation, science, or navigation services.
Q2: Why does a geostationary satellite appear fixed in the sky?
  1. Its equatorial orbital period matches Earth’s rotation ←
  2. Its polar orbit crosses the equator twice on every pass
  3. Its orbital period is shorter than Earth’s rotation
  4. It remains stationary without orbiting Earth
A circular equatorial orbit with the same rotational period as Earth keeps the satellite over one longitude.
Q3: Which country operates the Global Positioning System?
  1. India
  2. United States ←
  3. France
  4. Japan
GPS is the United States satellite-navigation constellation; other systems include India's NavIC and Europe's Galileo.
Q4: What did Chandrayaan-3 achieve in 2023?
  1. India’s first successful lunar soft landing ←
  2. India’s first Mars-orbit insertion
  3. India’s first solar-observation orbit
  4. India’s first crewed Earth-orbit mission
The Vikram lander achieved India's first soft landing on the Moon and deployed the Pragyan rover.
Q5: Which mission is designed as India's human-spaceflight programme?
  1. Chandrayaan-3
  2. Gaganyaan ←
  3. Aditya-L1
  4. Mangalyaan
Gaganyaan is intended to carry an Indian crew to low Earth orbit and return them safely.
Q6: Which feature defines a drone?
  1. It is an unmanned aircraft controlled remotely or autonomously ←
  2. It is a crewed aircraft flown only by an onboard pilot
  3. It is an unpowered balloon tethered to the ground
  4. It is a spacecraft maintained in Earth orbit
A drone operates within the atmosphere without an onboard human pilot, using remote control, automation, or both.
Q7: Which orbit-service match is false?
  1. Geostationary — fixed apparent position
  2. GPS — satellite navigation
  3. Drone — permanent Earth orbit ←
  4. Satellite — communication or observation
A drone is an aircraft in the atmosphere, not an orbital spacecraft; the other pairings describe standard space uses.
Q8: A drone follows programmed waypoints and returns to its launch site. Which external system likely supplies position?
  1. GPS ←
  2. Inertial measurement unit
  3. Visual odometry
  4. Barometric altimeter
Satellite-navigation fixes allow many drones to hold position, fly routes, and trigger return-to-home behaviour.
Q9: Which comparison with Gaganyaan is accurate?
  1. Chandrayaan-3 was robotic lunar exploration; Gaganyaan targets crewed Earth orbit ←
  2. Both missions are designed to place crews on the lunar surface
  3. Gaganyaan is a lunar rover while Chandrayaan-3 is an orbital crew capsule
  4. Chandrayaan-3 and Gaganyaan are both satellite-navigation constellations
The missions differ in destination and crew: Chandrayaan-3 was uncrewed and lunar, while Gaganyaan focuses on astronauts in low Earth orbit.
Q10: Which navigation constellation is not geostationary?
  1. GPS, whose satellites use medium Earth orbit ←
  2. A fixed communications satellite over the equator
  3. A weather satellite matching Earth's rotation
  4. A broadcast spacecraft stationed over one longitude
GPS satellites orbit in medium Earth orbit and move across the sky; geostationary satellites remain apparently fixed.
