Practice Questions

Q1: In which year was the United Nations founded?
  1. 1896
  2. 1945 ←
  3. 1950
  4. 1954
The UN was created in 1945 after the Second World War to support peace, cooperation, development, and international law.
Q2: When did the Constitution of India come into effect?
  1. 15 August 1947
  2. 26 November 1949
  3. 26 January 1950 ←
  4. 2 October 1950
The Constituent Assembly adopted the text on 26 November 1949, but commencement occurred on Republic Day, 26 January 1950.
Q3: Whose will established the Nobel Prizes?
  1. Pierre de Coubertin
  2. Alfred Nobel ←
  3. Andrew Carnegie
  4. Henry Dunant
Alfred Nobel directed most of his estate to prizes in named fields, creating the basis for the international awards.
Q4: Where were the first modern Olympic Games held in 1896?
  1. Paris
  2. Athens ←
  3. London
  4. Stockholm
Athens hosted the first modern Olympics in 1896, reviving an international festival inspired by the ancient Greek Games.
Q5: Which two seas does the Suez Canal connect?
  1. Black Sea and Caspian Sea
  2. Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea ←
  3. Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal
  4. Baltic Sea and North Sea
The Egyptian canal links the Mediterranean with the Red Sea, shortening the Europe-Asia sea route by avoiding Africa's southern tip.
Q6: What rank does the Bharat Ratna hold among Indian civilian awards?
  1. Second-highest Indian civilian award
  2. Highest Indian civilian award ←
  3. Highest Indian wartime gallantry award
  4. Highest Indian literary honour
Instituted in 1954, the Bharat Ratna is India's highest civilian honour for exceptional service or performance.
Q7: Which statement avoids confusing the UN with the Indian Constitution?
  1. The UN is an international organisation; the Constitution is India's supreme domestic law ←
  2. Both are the same 1950 statute
  3. The Constitution created every UN agency
  4. The UN administers the Bharat Ratna
The UN Charter governs an intergovernmental body, while India's Constitution structures national government and rights within India.
Q8: Which comparison with the Bharat Ratna is accurate?
  1. Both are Olympic medals
  2. Nobel Prizes are international awards; Bharat Ratna is an Indian civilian honour ←
  3. Both were created by the Indian Constitution
  4. Bharat Ratna follows Alfred Nobel's will
The awards differ in origin, scope, and selection even though both recognise exceptional human achievement.
Q9: Which event occurred first?
  1. Opening of the Suez Canal in 1869 ←
  2. First modern Olympics in 1896
  3. UN founding in 1945
  4. Indian Constitution commencement in 1950
The canal opened in 1869, followed by Athens 1896, the United Nations in 1945, and constitutional commencement in 1950.
Q10: Which claim about the Bharat Ratna is incorrect?
  1. It is India's highest civilian award
  2. It was instituted in 1954
  3. It was created by a specific article of the Constitution ←
  4. It recognises exceptional service
The government instituted Bharat Ratna by notification; no constitutional article creates the award.
