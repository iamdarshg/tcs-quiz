  2. 1968 ←

  4. 2004

Q6: Which Tata institutions are philanthropic bodies rather than operating companies?
  1. Tata Trusts ←
  2. Tata Consultancy Services
  3. Tata Motors
  4. Tata Steel
Tata Trusts fund social initiatives and also collectively hold a controlling interest in Tata Sons.
Q7: Which sequence is chronological?
  1. TCS founded, Jamsetji founds group, Ratan becomes chair
  2. Jamsetji founds group, TCS founded, Ratan becomes chair ←
  3. Ratan becomes chair, TCS founded, group founded
  4. Tata Trusts begin after TCS listing only
The group's nineteenth-century origin preceded TCS in 1968, which in turn preceded Ratan Tata's 1991 chairmanship.
Q8: Why is Tata Sons central to a TCS history question?
  1. TCS began as its division and it remains the promoter ←
  2. TCS acquired Tata Sons before becoming a division
  3. Tata Sons is the charitable trust that awards TCS grants
  4. TCS and Tata Sons have never had an ownership relationship
The 1968 operation originated inside Tata Sons, and Tata Sons continues to be the principal Tata-group promoter of TCS.
Q9: Which statement is the trap?
  1. Ratan Tata chaired Tata Sons
  2. Ratan Tata was the 1968 founder of TCS ←
  3. Ratan Tata later chaired major trusts
  4. Ratan Tata led group expansion
TCS was established within Tata Sons in 1968 under F. C. Kohli's early leadership; Ratan Tata did not found it.
Q10: How are Tata Trusts and Tata Sons related?
  1. The trusts collectively control Tata Sons through shareholding ←
  2. Tata Sons owns the trusts as operating subsidiaries

  4. The trusts and Tata Sons have no ownership connection
