Q1: Which authority administers India's Aadhaar identity system?
  1. ISRO
  2. UIDAI ←
  3. NPCI
  4. ONDC Ltd
The Unique Identification Authority of India issues and administers Aadhaar; NPCI operates UPI and ISRO leads space activity.
Q2: Which organisation developed the Unified Payments Interface?
  1. NPCI ←
  2. UIDAI
  3. Reserve Bank of India
  4. Department of Telecommunications
The National Payments Corporation of India developed UPI as an interoperable instant bank-payment system.
Q3: Which trio best represents India Stack capabilities?
  1. Identity, payments, and consent-based data exchange ←
  2. Search, social networking, and targeted advertising
  3. Tax assessment, land surveying, and weather forecasting
  4. Hardware manufacturing, chip fabrication, and cloud hosting
India Stack describes interoperable digital public infrastructure spanning identity, paperless services, payments, and data empowerment.

  1. UIDAI
  2. NPCI
  3. ISRO ←
  4. DigiLocker

Q7: Which statement corrects a common mix-up?
  1. Aadhaar is identity; DigiLocker manages digital documents ←
  2. Aadhaar is a payment rail; UPI assigns identity numbers
  3. DigiLocker is a commerce network; ONDC stores certificates
  4. ONDC authenticates biometrics; Aadhaar lists online sellers
Aadhaar establishes a unique identity and authentication capability, while DigiLocker handles electronic records and certificates.

  2. NEFT

Q9: How does Aadhaar relate to India Stack?

  2. It supplies the instant-payment messaging layer
  3. It supplies the consent-based financial-data exchange layer
  4. It supplies the open digital-commerce discovery layer

Q10: Which comparison between ONDC and UPI is sound?
  1. Both promote interoperability, for commerce and payments respectively ←
  2. ONDC settles bank transfers while UPI lists sellers and catalogues
