Practice Questions

Q1: Which three people founded Apple in 1976?
  1. Jobs, Wozniak, and Wayne ←
  2. Gates, Allen, and Bezos
  3. Page, Brin, and Watson
  4. Torvalds, Ritchie, and Moore
Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne formed Apple in 1976; Wayne soon sold his stake.
Q2: Microsoft was founded in 1975 by which pair?
  1. Larry Page and Sergey Brin
  2. Bill Gates and Paul Allen ←
  3. Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak
  4. Jeff Bezos and Andy Jassy
Bill Gates and Paul Allen founded Microsoft in 1975 around software for early personal computers.
Q3: PageRank research at Stanford is linked to the founders of which company?
  1. IBM
  2. Meta
  3. Google ←
  4. Apple
Larry Page and Sergey Brin developed the research that led to Google, with PageRank central to its early search approach.
Q4: Which company began in 1994 as Jeff Bezos's online bookstore?
  1. Microsoft
  2. Amazon ←
  3. eBay
  4. IBM
Amazon started with online books before expanding into broad commerce, logistics, devices, media, and cloud services.
Q5: A quiz clue mentions mainframes, enterprise computing, and a long research tradition. Which company fits?
  1. Digital Equipment Corporation
  2. IBM ←
  3. Oracle
  4. Hewlett-Packard
IBM is historically associated with enterprise hardware, especially mainframes, as well as corporate research.
Q6: Which relationship is accurate?
  1. Android is led by Google and used by many hardware makers ←
  2. Android is Apple's desktop-only system
  3. Android was IBM's original PC operating system
  4. Android is Amazon's bookstore brand
Google leads Android development, and manufacturers across the industry use it on their mobile devices.
Q7: Which company is older?
  1. Apple, founded in 1976 ←
  2. Google, founded in 1976
  3. Amazon, founded in 1975
  4. Android, founded before Microsoft
Among those choices Apple carries the 1976 founding date; Google and Amazon emerged in the 1990s.
Q8: Why is IBM relevant to Microsoft's personal-computer rise?
  1. IBM chose Microsoft to supply an operating system for the IBM PC ←
  2. IBM invented PageRank for Microsoft
  3. Microsoft manufactured IBM mainframes
  4. IBM created Android for Windows phones
The operating-system deal for the IBM PC gave Microsoft software a pivotal place in the expanding PC market.
Q9: Which company-product match is correct?
  1. Google — Android ←
  2. Apple — PageRank
  3. Amazon — Windows
  4. IBM — iOS
Android is the Google-led mobile platform; PageRank belongs to Google's origins, while Windows and iOS belong to Microsoft and Apple.
Q10: A retailer later becomes a major provider of rented servers and storage. Which expansion is being described?
  1. Microsoft into Azure
  2. Amazon into AWS ←
  3. Google into Google Cloud
  4. IBM into IBM Cloud
Amazon Web Services extended Amazon beyond retail by offering on-demand computing infrastructure to other organisations.
