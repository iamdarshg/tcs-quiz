Practice Questions

Q1: Which project falls most directly under civil engineering?
  1. Designing a bridge foundation ←
  2. Designing a jet-engine compressor
  3. Laying out a microprocessor circuit
  4. Formulating a new antibiotic
Civil engineering covers the design and maintenance of structures and infrastructure such as bridges, roads, water systems, and buildings.
Q2: Which source is naturally replenished on a human timescale?
  1. Coal seam
  2. Crude oil reservoir
  3. Sunlight ←
  4. Natural gas field
Sunlight is continuously replenished and supports solar generation, unlike finite fossil-fuel deposits formed over geological time.
Q3: What conversion occurs inside a solar photovoltaic cell?
  1. Sunlight directly to electricity ←
  2. Moving air directly to electricity
  3. Chemical fuel to heat and then electricity
  4. Electricity to stored chemical energy
Photovoltaic materials produce an electric current when illuminated, avoiding a mechanical turbine in the direct conversion step.
Q4: A rotor turns because moving air has kinetic energy. What output does the generator produce?
  1. Electrical energy ←
  2. Mechanical energy stored only in the blades
  3. Chemical energy in a fuel tank
  4. Solar radiation
The rotor and drive train convert wind motion into mechanical rotation, and the generator converts that rotation into electricity.
Q5: Which production route qualifies hydrogen as green?
  1. Electrolysis powered by renewable electricity ←
  2. Coal gasification without carbon controls
  3. Oil refining alone
  4. Natural-gas combustion without capture
Green hydrogen is made by splitting water in an electrolyser whose electricity comes from renewable generation.
Q6: What does LEED evaluate?
  1. Environmental performance of buildings ←
  2. Fuel economy of road vehicles
  3. Frequency stability of electricity grids
  4. Aerodynamic efficiency of turbine blades
LEED is a green-building rating framework covering areas such as energy, water, materials, sites, and indoor environmental quality.
Q7: How can civil engineering enable a wind farm?
  1. By designing foundations, roads, and grid-related works ←
  2. By designing the blade aerodynamics and rotor profile
  3. By programming the generator-control electronics
  4. By forecasting wind speed for daily market bids
Turbines require substantial engineered foundations, access infrastructure, drainage, and connections before electrical generation can operate safely.
Q8: A solar array powers an electrolyser. What product connects the two topics?
  1. Green hydrogen ←
  2. Green ammonia
  3. Synthetic methane
  4. Methanol
Renewable solar electricity can split water into hydrogen and oxygen, making the resulting hydrogen a green fuel feedstock.
Q9: Which comparison correctly distinguishes solar power from wind power?
  1. Solar PV converts light; wind turbines convert moving air ←
  2. Solar PV and wind turbines both require fuel combustion in the generator
  3. Wind turbines convert sunlight directly while solar panels convert air motion
  4. Solar PV produces mechanical shaft rotation before generating electricity
Solar cells use the photovoltaic effect, whereas turbines first capture wind's kinetic energy through rotating blades.
Q10: A building earns a sustainability rating. Which claim would be a trap?
  1. LEED assesses building performance
  2. Renewable energy may contribute to credits
  3. LEED itself generates electricity ←
  4. Civil engineers may help implement rated features
LEED measures and certifies design outcomes; it is a rating system, not a technology that produces power.
